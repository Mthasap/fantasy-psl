What Is Fantasy PSL?

Fantasy PSL is South Africa's dedicated fantasy football platform built specifically for the Betway Premiership — the top tier of South African professional football. Unlike international fantasy games that ignore the PSL, Fantasy PSL is built entirely around local football, featuring every player from all 16 Betway Premiership clubs.

The game is free to play, requires no downloads, and works on any device. Simply register, pick your 15-player squad within a R100 million budget, and watch your points grow every gameweek as your chosen players perform in real matches.

Fantasy PSL was created by football fans, for football fans. We are not affiliated with the Premier Soccer League, Betway, or any official football body — we are an independent fan platform dedicated to making PSL football more engaging for supporters across South Africa.

Select 15 players from across all 16 PSL clubs within a R100 million budget. You need at least 2 goalkeepers, 5 defenders, 5 midfielders, and 3 forwards.

Pick a captain to earn double points. Choose wisely — your captain's performance can make or break your gameweek.

About the Betway Premiership

The Betway Premiership (formerly the DStv Premiership and Absa Premiership) is the top division of South African professional football, featuring 16 clubs competing across a 30-gameweek season from August to May. The league is administered by the Premier Soccer League (PSL) and is home to iconic clubs including Mamelodi Sundowns, Orlando Pirates, Kaizer Chiefs, AmaZulu FC, and Cape Town City.

Fantasy PSL provides the only dedicated fantasy football experience built around this league, giving South African football fans a way to engage more deeply with every match, every player, and every gameweek of the Betway Premiership season.
