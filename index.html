Gameweek Deadline
How it works
1
The deadline closes 90 minutes before the first match of each gameweek. This prevents last-minute transfers based on team news.
2
Once locked, no transfers, captain changes or bench reordering are allowed until the gameweek ends.
3
Your points are calculated automatically as matches are played. Auto-substitutions apply if a player doesn't appear.
4
When the gameweek ends, the deadline resets for the next gameweek and you can make your 1 free transfer again.
Transfer Rules
You get 1 free transfer per gameweek (rolls over, max 5 banked). Extra transfers cost −4 points each.
