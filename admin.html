SEO Guide — Getting Found on Google

Each post you publish gets a unique URL at /news/[slug] that Google can index. Follow these tips to rank for PSL searches:

  • Use keywords like "PSL", "Betway Premiership", "Fantasy PSL" in your headline
  • Write at least 200 words in the article body — Google rewards longer content
  • Post within 1–2 hours of a match finishing for breaking news
  • Use the Tips category for transfer advice posts — these rank well for "PSL fantasy tips GW24" etc.
  • Add a cover image URL to get rich previews when shared on WhatsApp/Twitter

Posts are served with proper meta tags (title, description, og:image) so they preview correctly when shared.
